## <font face="Space Grotesk">✨ What is Nexora?</font>

Nexora is a modern communication and collaboration platform designed to bring conversations, communities, creativity, and everyday actions together in one place.

Instead of being only a place to send messages, Nexora is built around a simple idea:

> **Conversations should be able to become something useful.**

Nexora combines messaging, communities, collaboration, media sharing, planning, and future smart features into one connected experience.

The goal is to create a platform that feels:

- ⚡ **Fast**
- 🎨 **Modern**
- 📱 **Mobile-first**
- 💡 **Simple to use**
- ⚙️ **Customizable**
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Community-focused**
- 🛠️ **Useful for everyday life**

Nexora is designed to work seamlessly across devices while keeping conversations, communities, and activity perfectly synchronized.

---

# <font face="Space Grotesk">🚀 Core Features</font>

## <font face="Space Grotesk">💬 Direct Chats</font>

Nexora supports private one-to-one conversations with features such as:

- Text messaging
- Emoji support
- Message reactions
- GIFs
- Attachments
- Image sharing
- Rich link previews (including Pinterest & YouTube)
- Message pinning
- Quick actions
- Online and custom status

The goal is to make conversations feel lightweight while still supporting rich content.

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">🏠 Hubs</font>

Hubs are Nexora's community and group-space system. A Hub can be created around almost anything:

- 🎮 **Gaming**
- 📚 **Studying**
- 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 **Friends**
- 🏫 **Projects**
- ✈️ **Trips**
- 💡 **Communities**
- 🛠️ **Collaboration**

Each Hub can have its own identity and structure.

**Planned Hub customization includes:**
- Custom icon, banner, description, and tags
- Background customization
- Hub types & invite links
- Members & granular permissions management
- Dedicated Rooms

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">🚪 Rooms</font>

Rooms allow large Hubs to stay organized without putting every conversation into a single channel.

**Planned Room features include:**
- Room-specific conversations
- Member management
- Pinned content, files, and media
- Voice & video controls
- Tasks & events tracking
- Custom room settings

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">🎮 Gaming Hubs</font>

Gaming Hubs are specialized Hubs designed specifically around gaming communities.

**Key features include:**
- Game identity, icon, and banner customization
- Squad members & live gaming status
- Gaming sessions, goals, and achievements
- Clip sharing & game information
- Voice/call controls integrated directly into communication
- Gaming-specific Rooms alongside normal chat and GIF support

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">👤 Profiles</font>

Profiles are a central part of Nexora's identity system. Clicking a user's name or avatar opens their Nexora Profile.

**Users can customize:**
- Profile picture & banner
- Display name & username
- Bio & status
- Profile theme, frames, and featured Hub
- Social links

**Profiles display useful information such as:**
- Online & custom status
- Mutual Hubs & shared activity
- Shared media & friends list

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">🏠 Hub Profiles</font>

Hubs also feature dedicated public profiles containing:
- Hub icon, banner, name, description, and tags
- Hub type, member count, and online members list
- Owner and admin details
- Active Rooms, pinned content, shared media, events, and gaming activity

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">📱 Mobile-First Experience</font>

Nexora is designed with mobile users as a primary priority.

**Planned mobile features include:**
- Quick Actions & one-handed composer
- Voice messages & voice-to-text
- System-wide Share-to-Nexora sheet
- Quick camera & document sharing
- Smart media compression & low-data mode
- Offline message queue & automatic reconnection handling
- Actionable notifications & faster navigation across Hubs/Rooms
- Universal search

---

## <font face="Space Grotesk">⚡ Quick Actions</font>

Nexora uses a central Quick Actions system for fast navigation. The central `+` action button gives quick access to:

- **Chats:** New Chat • Photo • File • Voice
- **Hubs:** New Room • Photo • File • Event • Task
- **Rooms:** Voice • Photo • File • Task
- **Location Sharing**
